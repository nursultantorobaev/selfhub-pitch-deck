The Problem

Independent beauty, wellness, and health professionals are forced to be marketers, web designers, and tech experts.

Slow Digital Setup: Creating a professional digital presence takes 3–5 days.

Outdated Tools: Platforms like Booksy/Vagaro are complicated, outdated, and unintuitive.

Operational Pain: They struggle with service setup, pricing, scheduling, and payment connection.

Communication Drain: Client communication in WhatsApp/Instagram takes hours every day.

Lost Income: Missed messages result in lost income.

Onboarding Failure: Many professionals never finish onboarding and lose momentum.

The core insight: Professionals are great at their craft, but existing tools force them into roles they are not: marketers, web designers, or tech experts.

Solution — SelfHub AI

An AI-first, all-in-one platform designed specifically for independent beauty, wellness, and health professionals.

The 60-Second AI Onboarding

With one prompt, our AI instantly builds a complete digital business:

Complete business profile
Service list + pricing
Description, images, branding
Scheduling calendar
Payment setup (Stripe Connect)

This transforms 3–5 days of work into 60 seconds.

Additional AI Automation Layers

AI Marketing Assistant

Real-time business analytics; identifies slow days, trends, and revenue patterns; suggests promotions, pricing updates, and retention strategies.

Impact: Drives revenue and growth.

AI Customer Service Agent

Handles messages on WhatsApp, Instagram, and Messenger; books appointments automatically; answers FAQs.

Impact: Reduces up to 80% manual messaging.
